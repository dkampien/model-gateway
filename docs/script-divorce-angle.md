# Divorce angle: static teardown and adaptation into the winning video format

Companion to `winning-video-teardown.md`. That doc holds the format and the psychology of the winner. This doc holds the new angle (from the Notes-screenshot static) and the script that ports it into that format with minimal rewriting.

---

## 1. The static, as-is

**Format:** iOS Notes app screenshot, 9:16, black text on white, native chrome (9:41, signal, wifi, battery, "Notes" back button, "Done"). A black "START HERE" pill at the bottom is the only ad element.

**Copy, verbatim:**

> **My husband wants a divorce, I'm holding on to Jesus**
>
> I'm bringing this marriage back to **God.**
> If he told you he wants a divorce and you don't... if you're utterly devastated but you still won't give up and let the enemy win...
> Here's the truth. **You're not wrong to hold on.** You're not weak or foolish.
> It's just too heavy to carry alone. That's all.
> That's why I answered a few questions and put **what I'm carrying** into my own words.
> I'm not falling apart alone anymore. Not carrying this in the dark. I'm bringing it to God every day. The way He meant me to.
> It built me a private plan around exactly what I'm walking through. Something just for me. I did not expect to feel this held.
>
> **START HERE**

### Problem
A wife whose husband has told her he wants a divorce, who does not want one, and who is refusing to let go. The acute pain is not the divorce itself. It is **carrying an unbearable thing in secret, alone, while being quietly judged for not accepting it.**

### Angle
**Validation of holding on.** The ad's entire persuasive weight is on one line: *"You're not wrong to hold on. You're not weak or foolish."*

This is precisely the same psychological move as the winning video, aimed at a different accusation:

| | Winning video | This static |
|---|---|---|
| Accusation the viewer already believes about herself | "I keep failing the same test" | "I'm weak and foolish for not letting go" |
| Reframe | You are not failing, you are being trained | You are not weak, it is just too heavy for one person |
| Villain after the reframe | Search cost ("knowing where to find them") | Isolation ("carrying this in the dark") |
| Result | Shame becomes purpose | Shame becomes permission |

That correspondence is why this angle ports cleanly. The static already runs the winner's core mechanic.

### Why the static works
* **Native-format misdirection.** A Notes screenshot does not read as an ad. Same job the platformer animation does in the video: buy the first second by not looking like marketing. Also reads as forwarded, as if a friend sent it, which imports borrowed trust.
* **First person, never second person, until it turns.** "My husband." "I'm holding on." The reader is overhearing a confession, not being pitched. Then one sentence turns outward ("If he told you...") and the reader is recruited. Liking / Similarity Bias doing what Authority Bias cannot: nobody wants a expert on their marriage, they want someone who is in it.
* **The disclosure is disproportionate, which is why it is believed.** Nobody fabricates "my husband wants a divorce" to sell an app. Costly disclosure is a credibility signal.
* **Spiritual warfare in-group language.** "Let the enemy win" is a tribe marker. It filters hard for the target audience and signals Unity: written by one of us, not by a marketer studying us.
* **The product is never described, only its effect.** No features, no AI, no chat, no Bible database. "I answered a few questions" and "it built me a private plan." Effect-only description sidesteps every objection a feature list would invite.
* **Privacy is the feature.** "A private plan." "Something just for me." "Not carrying this in the dark." In a church community, divorce is the thing you cannot say out loud, so *private* is not a nice-to-have, it is the unlock.
* **"I did not expect to feel this held."** Surprise is a credibility device: an outcome the narrator did not anticipate cannot have been the pitch. And "held" is the exact emotional job to be done. Not fixed, not advised, not saved. Held.
* **CTA is "START HERE", not "download".** Same micro-ask logic as the winner: the ask is to begin, not to buy.
* **What is absent:** no promise the marriage is saved, no outcome claim, no price, no urgency, no scripture quoted at the reader. It promises *company inside the pain*, which is both unfalsifiable and the actual thing she wants.

### What the static is missing versus the video
1. **No authority beat.** Nothing answers "who built this and is it doctrinally safe." The video spends 6 full seconds on theologians / scholars / engineers.
2. **No visible product.** No UI, so no proof and no reduction of activation energy.
3. **No mechanism.** The video explains *why* the problem persists, which is what earns it the right to prescribe. The static substitutes validation for explanation.
4. **No finite container.** The video's "7 day plan" with steps closes the loop. The static's "private plan" is shapeless.

The adaptation below keeps everything in the static's favour and fills all four gaps from the video.

## 2. Decisions taken in the port

**Voice: keep first person, female.** The static's power is peer disclosure, and rewriting it into the winner's second-person male VO would throw away the single best asset (the confession) to gain a lever (authority) that the visuals can carry on their own. So the trust mechanism shifts from Authority Bias to Liking / Similarity, and the theologians beat stays intact but now lives mostly in the *picture* while she narrates. This is the one deliberate departure from the winner.

**Agitation beat becomes identification, not mechanism.** The winner explains a cause ("the test repeats"). There is no honest mechanism for "your husband wants a divorce," and inventing one would be both false and cruel. The slot is filled instead with the static's recruitment sentence ("if he's said that to you and you don't want out"). Narrower reach, much higher intent. Flagged as a deviation to watch.

**Repetition metaphor becomes a weight metaphor.** The winner's visual spine is the same bridge crossed three times, mirroring "the same test again." This angle's spine is *"too heavy to carry alone,"* so the wanderer now drags a load that grows across the three platformer shots. Same structure, same three-beat escalation, new payload. Form still mirrors message.

**Everything else is held constant:** 33s, 9:16, 11 shots, hard cuts with VO carrying across, dark to bright arc, faceless hooded wanderer, library rotunda, the scholars table, the phone rising from the glowing Bible, real UI composited in post, centered bold white subtitles in 1.0 to 1.5s chunks, all-caps end card phrased around "you".

### Audio

* **VO:** female, 30s to 40s, warm, quiet, close-miked and intimate. Composed but frayed at the edges, not crying. No commercial read, no rising sales cadence, no smile in the voice. Pauses held slightly longer than comfortable.
* **Music:** sparser than the winner. Solo piano with ambient pad, no acoustic strum. The register here is grief, not adventure. Enters at 00:00, ducks hard under VO, lifts only at 00:23.7 on the cut to the green valley.
* **SFX:** a single low wooden creak at 00:00 as the plank falls. A rope or stone drag under shots 2 and 3, getting heavier. Silence on the drop at 00:09.9. Magical synth rise at 00:20.5 as the phone leaves the book. Birds and water from 00:23.7.

## 4. What changed, what did not

**Kept from the static (near verbatim):** the headline as the hook, "if he told you he wants a divorce and you don't," "utterly devastated," "won't give up and let the enemy win," "you're not wrong to hold on, you're not weak or foolish," "too heavy to carry alone," "I answered a few questions," "what I'm carrying," "into my own words," "a private plan around exactly what I'm walking through," "not carrying this in the dark," "I did not expect to feel this held," and the START HERE button.

**Kept from the winner (structure and craft):** 6-beat spine and its exact timings, 33s, 11 shots, hard cuts with VO carrying across, "theologians, scholars and engineers" verbatim, the library and scholars and phone-from-Bible sequence as literal reused shots, the dark to bright arc, the faceless wanderer, real UI as proof, the 7 day finite plan, word-chunk subtitles, ducked music, micro-ask CTA, all-caps end card phrased around "you".

**Cut from the static:** "I'm bringing this marriage back to God" (redundant against the hook), "That's all" (works on the page, reads flat aloud), "I'm not falling apart alone anymore" (overlaps "not carrying this in the dark," kept the stronger one), "I'm bringing it to God every day, the way He meant me to" (no room, and the dashboard's daily steps show it), "Something just for me" (folded into the UI's privacy line).

**Added, filling the static's four gaps:** the authority beat (theologians, scholars and engineers, plus the scholars table shot), visible UI as proof and as activation-energy reduction, a finite container (7 day plan, Step 1, Step 2, Mark Complete), and community posts for social proof against the isolation.

## 5. Risks to watch

* **Reach is much narrower than the winner.** "The same situation over and over" is close to universal. "My husband wants a divorce and I don't" is a small slice of a slice. Expect a lower ceiling and a much higher rate among those it reaches. Do not read low reach as a losing creative; judge it on cost per install within the segment.
* **The peer-confession voice and the platformer animation are two different kinds of misdirection stacked on each other.** A confession this intimate over stylised game art may read as fictional and cost the credibility the static earns for free. This is the highest-risk element in the port. Mitigation to test as a variant: open on the Notes screenshot itself for the first 1.2s, then cut into the animation. That inherits the static's proven scroll-stopper and pre-authenticates the voice before the art style has to carry it.
* **Do not add an outcome promise.** The static never claims the marriage is saved and it must stay that way. Promising restoration would be both false and the fastest way to burn trust in this vertical.
* **Same discipline as the winner: no urgency, no scarcity, no price on screen.** Absence of pressure is part of why the format works.
* **One variable at a time.** This port changes VO gender, POV, angle, and visual metaphor at once. If it loses, the cause will not be readable. If budget allows, hold the winner's second-person male VO in one variant and change only the angle, so the POV shift is measured separately.
